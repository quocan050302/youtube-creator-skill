If a work-from-home job pays you to inspect and reship packages, stop before you sign anything. The listing looks routine: flexible hours and steady pay for checking boxes delivered to your doorstep. Here's the red flag—legitimate retailers don't run shipping hubs through a stranger's home address. In a reshipping scam, criminals typically buy goods with stolen payment details and ship them to you. You relabel and forward each box, putting your name and home address on the shipping trail while they stay hidden. When payday hits, the paycheck bounces or never arrives—leaving you facing fraud investigators or postal inspectors. Never accept a remote job routing packages through your house, verify employers through their official website, and report suspicious offers to official consumer-protection or postal authorities.
