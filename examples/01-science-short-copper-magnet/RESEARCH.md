## Analogy vs. Literal Mechanism Separation
- **Everyday Analogy:** Dragging a paddle through water (faster motion produces stronger pushback).
- **Literal Mechanism:** No fluid or physical contact exists; moving magnetic fields induce electric current loops (**eddy currents**) inside the conductive copper wall, generating an opposing magnetic field (Lenz's law).
- **Open Creator Check Before Filming:** Actual fall time depends on magnet strength, copper wall thickness, and inner clearance — do not state an exact fall duration in seconds unless timed on camera.
